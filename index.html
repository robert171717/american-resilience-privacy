American Resilience Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

American Resilience is a daily quote app with a widget and an optional reminder. It has hundreds of quotes: attributed classics, Bible verses, and American Resilience Originals written for the app, plus optional collections like Military & First Responders, Campaign Trail, and Militia and Liberty. It is not medical, legal, financial, or religious advice.

What stays on your device

We don’t collect personal information. Your favorites, settings, category choices, reminder times, streak, and Memorize progress and founder badges are saved on this iPhone. Memorize doesn’t use the microphone. The widget reads the day’s quote from a shared space on this iPhone that only the app and its widget can use. The quotes you move through with Next and Previous are kept only while the app is open. Read aloud uses the voices built into your iPhone, so the text stays on your phone. There’s no account, and we have no server that stores your library or favorites.

What we do not do

No ads. No analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own (if you share crash reports with developers in iOS Settings, Apple handles that). No tracking. We don’t build profiles, and we don’t sell or share your data for marketing. No account, and no cloud backup of your favorites through us.

When the app goes online

The app talks to Apple’s App Store to show prices, handle purchases and Restore Purchases, and check your subscription. It also sends one small check to apps.apple.com to see if you’re online, and that check carries no information about you. Source links and this page open in a browser view inside the app. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Sharing and email

Share cards are made on your iPhone. You choose where to send them with the iOS share sheet. If you email us, we get your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply.

Subscriptions

Optional Premium is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through Apple. Apple processes payment. You can manage or cancel in your Apple Account (Apple ID) subscriptions. Refund requests go through Apple’s Report a Problem flow; this app does not promise a refund.

Notifications

Reminders and streak nudges are optional local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. You can turn them off in Settings. We don’t send notifications from our own servers.

Questions: americanresilience@yahoo.com

American Resilience Terms of Use

Last updated: October 28, 2026

By using American Resilience you agree to these terms.

The app

Content is for personal, non-commercial inspiration. Quotes are attributed and provided as-is. We may update the quote library in app updates. Some collections, like Campaign Trail and Militia and Liberty, are part of Premium and stay off until you turn them on in Settings > Categories. Originals can be turned on or off there too.

No advice

Nothing in the app is professional advice. Scripture uses the World English Bible (public domain) where applicable.

Subscriptions

Premium is an optional auto-renewable subscription that unlocks the full library. It is offered as Premium Annual (renews every year) or Premium Monthly (renews every month). Prices are shown in the app before you buy. There’s no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account (Apple ID) when you confirm the purchase. Your subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple Account settings or the App Store. If you reinstall the app or get a new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases to turn Premium back on with the same Apple Account. If Apple can’t renew your payment, Premium stays on during Apple’s billing grace period while Apple retries.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse, reverse engineer, or redistribute the app’s paid content outside normal personal use and iOS share features.
