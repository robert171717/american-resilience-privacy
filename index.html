American Resilience Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

American Resilience is a daily quote, widget, and reminder app with hundreds of quotes: attributed classics, including Bible verses, and American Resilience Originals written for the app. Categories are Faith, Discipline, Resilience, Work, Grit, Courage, America, Leadership, and Military & First Responders. It is not medical, legal, financial, or religious advice.

What stays on your device

The app does not collect any personal data. Quotes you browse, category choices, favorites, settings, reminder time, and subscription status flags used by the app stay on this device. Widgets read the day’s quote from this device only. There is no account and no American Resilience server that stores your library or favorites.

What we do not do

No ads. No analytics. No tracking. No selling personal data. No account creation. No cloud backup of your favorites through us.

Subscriptions

Optional Premium is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through Apple. Apple processes payment. You can manage or cancel in your Apple Account (Apple ID) subscriptions. Refund requests go through Apple’s Report a Problem flow; this app does not promise a refund.

Notifications

A daily local reminder is optional. You can turn it off in Settings. Notifications stay on-device; we do not push from our own servers.
