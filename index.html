American Resilience Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

American Resilience is a daily quote, widget, and reminder app (faith, sports, political leaders, classics). It is not medical, legal, financial, or religious advice.

What stays on your device

Quotes you browse, theme preferences, favorites, reminder time, and subscription status flags used by the app stay on this device. There is no account and no American Resilience server that stores your library or favorites.

What we do not do

No ads. No tracking SDKs for advertising. No selling personal data. No account creation. No cloud backup of your favorites through us.

Subscriptions

Optional Premium is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through Apple. Apple processes payment. You can manage or cancel in your Apple Account subscriptions. Refund requests go through Apple’s Report a Problem flow; this app does not promise a refund.

Notifications

A daily local reminder is optional. You can turn it off in Settings. Notifications stay on-device; we do not push from our own servers.
